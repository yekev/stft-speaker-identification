## Course provenance

The original ECE3001 assignment provided a starter scaffold and specified the
RAVDESS speaker-identification task. The submitted coursework contribution
focused on experiment configuration and execution, STFT hop-length ablation,
evaluation, visualization, and analysis. This repository is a separately
organized portfolio implementation and does not present the supplied course
scaffold as original work.
